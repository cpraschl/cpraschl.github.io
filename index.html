About Me

I am an assistant professor at the University of Applied Sciences Upper Austria (Campus Hagenberg) and research project manager in the AIST research group. My research focuses on computer vision – in particular drone-based wildlife monitoring, which is also the topic of my doctoral studies at the Institute of Computational Perception at Johannes Kepler University Linz. In addition, I teach courses on databases, image processing and web technologies at several universities. Beyond research and teaching, I spend my time with drones, 3D printing, graphic design and photography.

Highlights

BAMBI QGIS Plugin

Open-source QGIS plugin for drone-based wildlife surveys: detection, tracking and geo-referencing of wildlife in RGB and thermal footage, orthomosaic generation, and analysis and manual review of the results directly in the GIS.

BAMBI Dataset

Open multimodal dataset of nadir RGB and thermal drone recordings of forest wildlife, with annotated animals for detection and classification (NeurIPS 2026 – Evaluations and Datasets Track).
